Our story

Useful things,
made with care.

NovaShop started with a simple question: why are the objects we use every day so often an afterthought?

The short version

A small shop for the things you reach for.

We work with small makers to build quiet, durable pieces for the desk, the kitchen and the space in between. Nothing loud. Nothing disposable.

Every product starts with a material we like and a problem we have actually had. Then we make a short run, test it in real life and keep only what earns its place.

NovaShop is based online, ships from a small studio and is currently powered by a static website hosted on AWS S3.
